About Maskarwadi Foundation

Est. 2026

Our Story

Maskarwadi Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organization dedicated to environmental conservation, social welfare, and sustainable village development. Founded by a group of passionate friends, the foundation works to improve village life through environmental protection, community engagement, and development initiatives.

We believe real change begins at the grassroots level. By bringing together local communities, volunteers, and supporters, we create lasting impact that transforms lives and protects our environment.

Our Journey

Timeline

January 2026

Foundation Established

Eight friends united by a shared passion for environmental and social change officially founded Maskarwadi Foundation.

February 2026

First Tree Plantation Drive

Organized the first community tree plantation drive with local villagers, planting over 30 saplings along village roads and public lands.

March 2026

Tree Protection Initiative

Launched the tree protection program, building protective barriers around young saplings to ensure their healthy growth and survival.

Present & Future

Vision Ahead

Working towards village entrance gate, children's playground, solar street lighting, and comprehensive village development projects.
