Our Story

Maskarwadi Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organization dedicated to environmental conservation, social welfare, and sustainable village development. Founded by a group of passionate friends from Maskarwadi village, the foundation works to improve village life through environmental protection, community engagement, and development initiatives.

Growing up in Maskarwadi, the founders witnessed both the beauty of nature and the challenges faced by rural communities. This inspired them to take action — starting with simple tree plantation drives that soon grew into a full-fledged community movement.

Today, the foundation brings together local villagers, youth, and volunteers to create meaningful change. Every project is planned with the community, for the community.

What We Do

Our Initiatives

Tree Plantation Drives

Organizing large-scale tree plantation campaigns to increase green cover, restore ecosystems, and protect nature across the region. Each drive involves local villagers and volunteers working side by side.

Environment

Tree Protection

Building protective stone barriers around newly planted trees to ensure healthy growth and high survival rates. This simple but effective technique has significantly improved our plantation success.

Conservation

Environmental Awareness

Promoting environmental responsibility through school programs, community workshops, and public campaigns. We educate villagers about waste management, water conservation, and sustainable practices.

Education

Community Participation

Bringing villagers, youth, and volunteers together through collective action. We organize community meetings, cleanliness drives, and development planning sessions to ensure everyone has a voice.
